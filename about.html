Chloe: Your Partner in Tech & Creative Endeavors

Hello! I'm Chloe, the founder and primary consultant at Chloe's Technical and Creative Services. My journey into the world of technology and creative media has always been driven by a deep curiosity and a passion for making things work better, connect seamlessly, sound clearer, and run smoother.

For years, I've been honing my skills across a diverse landscape – from the intricacies of IT systems and advanced wireless network optimization to the delicate art of electronics repair and modification; from the creative precision of audio engineering to the logic of coding and automation. This broad experience, including specialized knowledge in wireless propagation and network administration, allows me to see the bigger picture and offer comprehensive solutions that truly fit your needs, whether you're an individual looking for personal tech help or a small business aiming to optimize your operations and connectivity.

Based in the vibrant community of Milwaukee, Wisconsin, I'm proud to offer my services both locally and, for many projects, remotely. My approach is built on clear communication, meticulous attention to detail, and a genuine desire to help you overcome your technological hurdles and achieve your goals. I believe that technology should be an enabler, not a barrier, and I'm here to ensure it works perfectly for you.

I understand that not everyone speaks "tech," and that's perfectly okay! My goal is to provide expert service in a way that's easy to understand, making sure you feel comfortable and informed every step of the way.

Let's work together to make your tech work for you!
